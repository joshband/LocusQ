Scope Guardrails

Keep the plugin centered on scene truth and the companion centered on device trust. Every visible control should earn its place by resolving a real uncertainty fast.

01No new always-visible control unless it removes ambiguity within five seconds.
02Diagnostics summarize by default and explain on demand.
03Device-bound capture and trust workflows stay in the companion.
04If a feature does not strengthen Calibrate, Emitter, Renderer, or Trusted Head Tracking, it is a side quest.
